League-Start Builds

Six builds picked for surviving Acts 1–10 and the first map tiers on league-start gear — no mirror-tier uniques required. Each leans on one attribute archetype so you can pick by class fantasy or by what's cheapest to gear.

Showing 6

Chieftain Righteous Fire

Marauder → Chieftain
BudgetLow
Strength Righteous Fire Tank / DoT

Self-cast Righteous Fire stacking life and fire regen — Chieftain's flat regen and fire exposure turn your own burn into your main defense layer as well as your damage.

Why league start: almost no unique-dependent, clears acts safely, and the "regen tank" playstyle forgives under-leveled gear.

Pathfinder Toxic Rain

Ranger → Pathfinder
BudgetLow
Dexterity Toxic Rain Chaos DoT

Toxic Rain pods stack chaos damage over time from range, while Pathfinder's permanent flask uptime covers evasion, curse immunity, and elemental ailment cleanse.

Why league start: chaos damage bypasses most early energy shield mobs, and the playstyle stays completely safe — you're never in melee range.

Hierophant Arc Totems

Templar → Hierophant
BudgetLow
Intelligence Arc Totem

Two Arc totems chain lightning across packs while you kite. Hierophant's extra totem and reduced totem cooldown makes this one of the safest levers in the game.

Why league start: totems take the hits instead of you — ideal for a first character or anyone learning boss patterns.

Berserker Boneshatter

Duelist → Berserker
BudgetLow–Med
Strength Boneshatter Melee

Boneshatter converts a slice of the hit you take into stacked bleed, and Berserker's Rage mechanic scales both damage and life-cost recoup as you keep swinging.

Why league start: fast acts clear speed for players who want melee impact instead of a totem or DoT playstyle.

Necromancer Skeletal Legion

Witch → Necromancer
BudgetLow
Intelligence Summon Skeletons Minion

Skeletal Warriors and Raging Spirits do the fighting while Necromancer's minion damage and life multipliers make them scale into maps on their own gear, not yours.

Why league start: extremely low gear requirements — most of the build's power comes from the ascendancy, not your item slots.

Deadeye Lightning Arrow

Ranger → Deadeye
BudgetMed
Dexterity Lightning Arrow Projectile

Lightning Arrow's chaining projectiles clear whole screens once you pick up a projectile-count source, and Deadeye's extra projectiles and Far Shot push it further.

Why league start: the highest early clear-speed ceiling on this list — best if you're comfortable managing evasion instead of raw armour.
