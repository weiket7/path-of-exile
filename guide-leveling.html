03 · Guide

Leveling: Act 1 → Act 10

The campaign hasn't changed shape in years — five milestone bands cover the whole run, from the Twilight Strand to the second Kitava fight. Tap a band to expand it.

Gem priorityTake every skill-gem quest reward over vendor gems — questing gems are free and often better than what you'd buy.
ResistancesYou don't need to cap 75% until you reach maps. Keep an eye on the character sheet from Act 6 onward.
Respec pointsSeveral quests across the acts grant passive skill refund points — save them for after you settle your leveling gem setup.
A1–A2

The Twilight Strand to the Vaal Ruins

Learn the acts, pick up your first support gems Lv 1–22
  • Clear side areas early — extra monster density is worth more XP than it costs in time while you're under-leveled.
  • Pick up a movement skill gem the moment it's offered; it pays for itself immediately.
  • Sell matching low-level items in sets of four to five for chance/alteration/transmutation orbs via vendor recipes — cheap early currency.
Boss note: Merveil (end of Act 1) alternates a melee tentacle slam with a projectile wave — walk through the gap in the wave rather than running from it. Vaal Oversoul (end of Act 2) splits into three elemental clones near death; focus one down fast so its damage doesn't stack.
A3–A4

Sarn to Highgate

First real damage checks, first six-link odds Lv 22–40
  • This is where a lot of new characters first feel "squishy" — it's usually a missing resistance, not a build problem. Check your character sheet.
  • Piety fights twice on the way through Sarn with different elemental themes each time; swap in whatever resistance flask you're carrying.
  • Grab your first jewel socket and start slotting a cheap early jewel once you have one.
Boss note: Dominus (end of Act 3) telegraphs a large slam — dodge it rather than tanking, since it hits far harder than anything before it. Malachai (end of Act 4) has a beam attack that tracks — keep moving instead of stopping to attack.
A5

Highgate to the Templar Courts

The first Kitava confrontation Lv 40–45
  • Shortest act on this list, but the gear you're wearing going in matters — you fight Kitava directly and lose, by design, so don't overspend on new gear right before it.
  • This is a natural checkpoint to do a passive respec if your early leveling tree drifted from your final build.
Boss note: you are not meant to win this fight — surviving long enough matters more than damage. The story continues in Act 6.
A6–A9

The corrupted continent

Same geography, harder monsters, new side content Lv 45–68
  • You retrace Wraeclast under Kitava's corruption — familiar zones, noticeably tougher packs, and a second wave of skill-gem quest rewards.
  • League-specific side content (Einhar's beasts, Alva's temple, Niko's mine, and similar) starts appearing here — all optional, all worth a quick detour for extra currency and crafting materials.
  • Start actively watching resistances now — by Act 9 you want all three capped or close to it before you touch Act 10.
Boss note: difficulty ramps steadily rather than in one big spike — if a specific zone feels unfair, it's almost always a resistance or life-pool gap.
A10

Oriath and the second Kitava fight

Campaign finale — unlocks the Atlas of Worlds Lv 68+
  • Confirm all resistances are capped and you have a flask up for whatever ailment your build is weakest to before starting the final fight.
  • Once Kitava is down, your first map drops automatically and the Atlas becomes available — head to the Atlas guide next.
Boss note: this fight has multiple phases with escalating area attacks — this is where a real defensive layer (not just high life) starts to matter for the first time.
