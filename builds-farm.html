Farm Builds

Endgame setups for once your league starter has capped out — built around a specific farm target rather than "does everything." Expect more currency invested than the league-start list.

Showing 6

Trickster Blade Vortex

Shadow → Trickster
BudgetMedium
Blade Vortex Full Clear

Blade Vortex's overlapping hits shred packed maps, and Trickster's Ghost Dance plus movement speed keep you weaving through Delirium fog or juiced rare packs without slowing down.

Why farm this: among the fastest full-map-clear speeds available, which is what actually drives currency-per-hour on juiced maps.

Elementalist Spark Stacking

Witch → Elementalist
BudgetMedium
Spark Elemental

Elementalist's golem stacking and penetration nodes push Spark's chaining projectiles into both dense pack clear and respectable single-target — a rare hybrid for a caster.

Why farm this: golem uptime keeps scaling passively, making it forgiving in the visual chaos of a fully juiced Delirium mirror.

Assassin Explosive Arrow

Shadow → Assassin
BudgetHigh
Explosive Arrow Single Target

Stacking Explosive Arrow fuses on a boss detonates for enormous single-hit damage — Assassin's crit multiplier and Elusive uptime turn it into one of the hardest-hitting bow setups against uber bosses.

Why farm this: built specifically for boss rotations (Maven, Elder, Shaper-tier) rather than trash clear — pair it with a separate map-clear skill if you juice maps too.

Occultist Bane / Contagion

Witch → Occultist
BudgetMedium
Bane Chaos DoT

Contagion spreads Essence Drain's damage-over-time across a whole pack in one cast, and Occultist's curse effect and chaos damage bonuses make Bane a one-button clear button.

Why farm this: chaos DoT ramps up over a second or two, which reads great against juiced rare-density maps where everything is dying anyway.

Slayer Cyclone

Duelist → Slayer
BudgetMedium
Cyclone Melee Leech

Slayer's overleech and "cannot be stunned while leeching" turn Cyclone into a nearly unkillable spin-to-win, sustained entirely by the corpses you're already deleting.

Why farm this: extremely low attention-cost per map — good pick if you're farming currency over long juiced sessions and want to not think about defense.

Champion Lightning Strike

Duelist → Champion
BudgetHigh
Lightning Strike Tanky Hybrid

Champion's permanent Fortify and taunt make this the build you bring when you want map clear and boss damage from the same six-link, at the cost of a lower raw ceiling than specialists above.

Why farm this: the safest all-rounder here — good for pushing juiced maps with dangerous mods without swapping skills for the boss room.
